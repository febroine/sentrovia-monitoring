# Shot list — "One Page" (30.0 s · 900 frames · 30 fps · 1920×1080, plus native 1080×1920 crop)

Frame ranges are half-open `[in, out)`. "Payoff" = the new visual idea the shot delivers (one every 3–5 s).
Vertical crop: each shot names its focus x (world px) — the 607-px-wide world strip that the vertical
render shows, re-rendered natively at 1080×1920 (no upscaling).

## S1 — Hook: the eye (0–60)
- **Frame:** extreme close-up of her left eye filling ~70 % of the frame. Cel skin, painted iris
  with concentric rings (ref3 grammar), one specular dot. Brows and bangs cut the top edge.
- **Reflection:** a convex-warped email card in the iris: `Re: Application — Junior Designer` /
  `Unfortunately, we have decided to move forward with other candidates.` A red notification dot.
- **Light:** red rim light on lid and cheek pulses 3× (at 0.15 s, 0.85 s, 1.55 s), synced to the notification tone.
- **Camera:** slow push-in 1.00 → 1.06.
- **Motion:** pupil contracts slightly on each pulse; at 1.70 s the eye starts to close (blink) → on frame 60 the lids are shut → hard cut to S2 (the blink is the cut).
- **SFX:** low drone, three soft "notification" sine blips (E5, slightly detuned), a held breath.
- **Payoff (0.0 s):** the rejection, read in her eye. **Vertical focus x:** 960.

## S2 — Act 1: river of résumés (60–210)
- **Frame:** wide. Sky in bands: indigo with sparse stars → blue band → burnt-red horizon with a black
  treeline and tiny red lights (ref4 grammar). The river is made of **paper ribbons**: long curving
  bands of overlapping cream pages flowing from the lower-right foreground toward the horizon,
  some with red `REJECTED` stamps and faint text lines. Silver agave and a few grass stalks on the left foreground bank.
- **Her:** small (≈ 260 px tall above water), waist-deep, three-quarter back view, slightly right of centre, leaning into the flow, arms raised to push pages aside (animated on twos).
- **Events:** at 3.6 s and 5.2 s a page near her catches, **tears in two**, and the halves darken and **sink**.
- **Camera:** slow drift right-to-left, 90 px, eased.
- **SFX:** water bed, continuous paper rustle, two tears (sync to tear frames), soft low piano minor seconds.
- **Payoff (2.0 s):** the river is paper. (4.6 s): pages tear and sink. **Vertical focus x:** 1060 (her).

## S3 — She grabs a page; the ink slides off (210–360)
- **Frame:** over-the-shoulder insert: her hands (cel) hold a wet page up against the red horizon; the
  page fills ~45 % of frame. The page is *her* old résumé: `MIRA AYDIN` / `Objective: hard-working team player seeking…` / bullet lines.
- **Events:** 7.4 s grab (page rises into frame, flow continues behind). 8.3–10.4 s the text **slides off like wet ink**: letters drift down, smear into long drips, and run off the bottom edge into the river. 10.6 s her fingers open; 10.8–12.0 s the page floats away right and sinks.
- **Camera:** slow push-in 1.00 → 1.05.
- **SFX:** wet paper grab, ink-drip trickles (filtered noise with falling pitch), the page sliding away.
- **Payoff (8.5 s):** the words don't stay. **Vertical focus x:** 960 (the page).

## S4 — Turning point: she sits (360–480)
- **Frame:** wide-medium on the bank. Dense painted botanicals (agave, dark leaves, a few night-pink blooms) frame the left; she sits **cross-legged** on a flat stone bank, left of centre, facing camera three-quarter; the river runs behind her to the red horizon on the right.
- **Events:** 12.0 s she has just sat down (settling, on twos). 12.6 s **exhale**: shoulders drop, eyes close, a small breath. The river's flow speed decays to ~10 % by 14.5 s; the first stars brighten in the indigo band.
- **Camera:** slow pull-back 1.06 → 1.00.
- **SFX:** water fades out over 0.8 s; one breath at 12.6 s; then **near silence** (faint room tone) until 16.0 s.
- **Payoff (12.0 s):** stillness. (14.5 s): stars appear, river stops. **Vertical focus x:** 760 (her).

## S5 — Act 2: words grow like plants (480–660)
- **Frame:** close: her lap and hands from slightly above; a blank cream sheet on her knees fills the frame centre; cel hands at the edges; bank foliage soft behind.
- **Events:** 16.3 s she smooths the blank sheet. Lines **grow** left-to-right as vines (a stem draws, the
  letters unfurl from it), each line ending in an **alder leaf** that sprouts in green:
  - 16.8 s `Mira Aydın` (Alegreya Medium, large) — leaf at 17.5 s
  - 18.3 s `I draw systems people can read.` (Alegreya Italic) — leaf at 18.9 s
  - 19.5 s `Illustration · Type · Figma` — leaf at 20.0 s
  - 20.5 s `Rebuilt my town library's signs, 2025.` — leaf at 21.1 s
- **Camera:** slow push-in 1.00 → 1.07 toward the page.
- **SFX:** soft pencil-scratch grains while each line grows; one piano note per leaf (C5, E5, G5, A5).
- **Payoff (16.8 s):** words grow. (17.5 s): the alder leaf. (21.1 s): the page is full. **Vertical focus x:** 960.

## S6 — The page becomes the screen (660–810)
- **6a (22.0–24.4 s), wide:** she sits on the bank (as S4 but reframed), the finished page **lifts** from her knees, rises, glows softly (warm cream halo). Behind her the river **turns into a quiet starry sea**: red ribbons cool to indigo, star reflections appear in the bands, the horizon red narrows to a thin line.
  Camera: slow tilt up following the page (60 px).
- **6b (24.4–27.0 s), insert:** the glowing page fills frame and **resolves into the aldercv.com CV**: paper texture eases into a clean page inside a minimal brand frame (teal `#123D45` bar with the alder logo and `aldercv.com`). One clean CV: name, one-line summary, Skills, Experience — calm, readable. No laptop, no device.
  Camera: slow push-in 1.00 → 1.04.
- **SFX:** shimmer as it lifts; pad swells; **the warm chord** lands at 24.5 s on the CV reveal.
- **Payoff (22.5 s):** the page rises. (23.5 s): the sea turns to stars. (24.5 s): it's a real CV on aldercv.com. **Vertical focus x:** 960.

## S7 — Tagline; she looks up (810–870)
- **Frame:** medium close-up of her face and shoulders against the starry sea; the page glow lights her face from below-front in cream; one alder leaf in her hair clip.
- **Text:** `Your story, one page.` (Alegreya, cream) then `aldercv.com` (Figtree SemiBold, with the logo mark), lower third, held ≥ 1.3 s.
- **Events:** 27.0 s eyes lowered; 27.9 s she **looks up at the camera** (on twos), a small calm expression.
- **Camera:** very slow push 1.00 → 1.03.
- **SFX:** chord tail, one soft bell on the URL.
- **Payoff (27.0 s):** the line + the URL. **Vertical focus x:** 960 (text relaid for vertical).

## S8 — Cut to black, then the calm eye (870–900)
- 870–878: **black** (cut).
- 878–900: the S1 framing exactly, but calm: lids relaxed, the iris reflects the **green-lit CV page** with a tiny leaf; indigo starry light instead of red. Holds still. The last frame matches S1's framing so the loop cut green → red reads as one eye.
- **SFX:** chord tail ends; one high soft piano note at 878.
- **Payoff (29.3 s):** same eye, new reflection. **Vertical focus x:** 960.
